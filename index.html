“When reversing, your front bumper swings outward—always check your outside front corner before committing to full lock.”

90° Reverse Bay: Pull past the target bay until your rear axle aligns with the near edge of the bay. Turn full lock toward the bay, watch the dashed gold trajectory arc, and straighten the wheel as soon as your body is parallel.

Tight Parallel Park: Pull alongside the front car, reverse at full lock until your car reaches a 45° angle, then counter-steer full lock the opposite way so your front nose tucks cleanly behind the lead car without clipping the curb.
